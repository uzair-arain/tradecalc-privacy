Privacy Policy — TradeCalc (Construction Calculator Offline)

Effective date: 31 October 2026
App: TradeCalc — Construction Calculator Offline (Android package com.uzair.tradecalc)

Summary

TradeCalc works fully offline. It does not collect, store on any server, sell or share your personal data.

No account and no internet access

You do not need an account to use the app. This version does not request internet access, so it cannot send data over the network.

What is stored on your device

The app stores one setting on your device: a yes/no value showing that you have accepted the in-app disclaimer. It is not sent anywhere. You can delete it by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.

Your calculation inputs and results are not saved by the app.

Sharing results

If you tap Share, the app passes the result as text to your device's share sheet. The text leaves your device only if you choose a share target (for example, a messaging or email app). After that, the privacy policy of the app you chose applies. We do not receive a copy.

Ads, analytics and purchases

This version has no advertising, analytics, crash reporting or in-app purchases.

Sale of data

We do not sell your data.

Children

TradeCalc is a general-audience tool for construction work. It is not directed at children under 13.

Future changes

We plan to add advertising and an optional paid "Pro" upgrade in a future version. Before that version is released, we will update this policy to explain any data those features use. The updated policy will have a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy: contact via the Play Store listing for this app.
